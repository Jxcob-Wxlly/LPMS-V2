# LPMS app - user guide (teachers and parents)

For READ Educational Trust teachers. You do not need any technical knowledge. Screenshots go in `docs/images/` (see the
**Screenshot** notes) and are embedded in the submission document.

---

## 1. Before you start

You need:

- an Android phone (Android 8.0 or newer) with mobile data or Wi-Fi;
- the LPMS Teacher app installed (ask your coordinator for the file, or see section 9);
- your **email address or username** and **password** from your administrator.

## 2. Signing in

![Screenshot: sign-in screen](images/01-sign-in.png)

1. Open **LPMS Teacher**.
2. In **Email or username**, type the address or username you were given.
3. Type your **Password**. Tap **Show** to see what you typed and **Hide** to cover it again.
4. Tap **Sign in**.

If something is wrong the app tells you in plain words and keeps what you typed:

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Enter your email address or username." / "Enter your password." | The box was left empty. Fill it in. |
| "Incorrect email/username or password." | Check for typing mistakes, then try again. |
| "This account is inactive. Please contact your administrator." | Your account has been switched off. Contact your administrator. |
| "Too many attempts. Please wait a minute and try again." | Wait a minute, then try again. |
| "Can't reach the server..." | See section 8. |

The app can remember your **username** (never your password). Turn this on or off in Settings.

### Creating an account (new teachers)

1. On the sign-in screen tap **Create an account**.
2. Fill in **Your name** (first and last name), **Email**, choose **School**, and pick a **Password** (at least 8 characters with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter and a number).
3. Tap **Create account**. You are signed in straight away.

If the email is already registered, tap **Sign in** instead.

## 3. The four main screens

The bar at the bottom of the screen switches between **Home**, **Sessions**, **Learners** and **Settings**.

### 3.1 Home

![Screenshot: home screen](images/02-home.png)

Shows a greeting, your school and four numbers: **Sessions this week**, **Sessions today**, **Learners** and **Attendance rate**.
If any finished sessions still have no attendance, an orange box tells you how many and has a **View sessions** button.
Below that, **Today's sessions** lists what you teach today - tap one to take attendance.
Tap the circular arrow at the top right to refresh.

### 3.2 Sessions

![Screenshot: sessions list](images/03-sessions.png)

Choose **Upcoming**, **Past** or **All** at the top. Each card shows the programme, school, date and time, and a status:

- **Upcoming** (blue) - not finished yet;
- **Attendance needed** (orange) - the session is over but attendance has not been recorded;
- **Completed** (green) - attendance recorded, with the number present and absent.

Tap a card to open it.

**Scheduling a session.** Tap **+ New** (or **New session** on Home), choose the programme, the date, and the start and end times, then tap **Save session**. Dates in the past and dates outside the programme are not allowed, and the end must be after the start. The session appears under Upcoming.

### 3.3 Taking attendance

![Screenshot: attendance screen](images/04-attendance.png)

1. Open a session. You see the learners at the school.
2. For each learner tap **Present** or **Absent** (the chosen button gets a tick and a colour).
3. To save time tap **All present**, then change only the learners who were away.
4. The line under the session shows how many are present, absent and not yet marked.
5. When every learner is marked, tap **Save attendance**. A message confirms it was saved.

Good to know:

- You can only take attendance **on the day of the session or afterwards**; future sessions are read-only.
- You can come back and **correct** attendance later, then save again.
- If you leave with unsaved marks the app asks whether to discard them.
- You can only open **your own** sessions.

### 3.4 Learners

![Screenshot: learners list](images/05-learners.png)

The list shows the learners at your school with their **average score** and **attendance**.
Type part of a first or last name in **Search by name** to narrow the list; tap the cross to clear it.
Tap a learner to see their progress.

### 3.5 A learner's progress

![Screenshot: learner progress](images/06-learner-progress.png)

You see the learner's name, grade, a plain-language progress label
(for example *Good progress*), the average score and attendance, a list of **assessments** and their **recent attendance**.

**Add an assessment**

1. Tap **Add assessment**.
2. Enter the **Score** from 0 to 100 (for example `72` or `72.5`).
3. Tap **Assessment date** and choose a date (today or earlier).
4. Add **Notes** if you want (up to 255 characters).
5. Tap **Save**.

**Edit or delete** - tap the pencil or the bin on an assessment **you** recorded. Assessments recorded by other teachers
are read-only. Deleting asks you to confirm first.

The form checks your entries and shows a red message under any box that needs fixing, for example
"The score must be between 0 and 100." or "The assessment date can't be in the future."

## 3b. For parents and guardians

Parents sign in with the username and password their school gave them (for example `parent01`). After signing in you land on **My children**.

- **My children** lists each of your children with their average score and attendance, and a coloured tag showing whether attendance
  **meets** or is **below** the expectation you set.
- Tap a child to open the **report**: a plain-language progress label, every assessment with its date, score and the teacher's note,
  and the most recent attendance.
- Tap **Download PDF report** (or the share icon at the top). Your phone's share sheet opens so you can save the PDF to Files or Drive,
  print it, or send it by WhatsApp or email.
- In **Settings > Attendance expectation**, drag the slider (50% to 100%) to set the attendance you expect. The app compares your
  child's attendance with it on the list, the report and in the PDF. The default is 80%.
- You can only see your own children. If you are not linked to a learner yet, the app says so; please contact READ Educational Trust.

Teachers can also create this PDF: open a learner's progress screen and tap the share icon at the top.

## 4. Settings

![Screenshot: settings](images/07-settings.png)

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Account** | Shows who is signed in. **Change password** and **Sign out** are here. |
| **Theme** | Use phone setting, Light or Dark. |
| **Larger text** | Makes all text about 20% bigger. |
| **Remember my username** | Fills in your username on the sign-in screen next time. |
| **Server** | The web address of the LPMS server. Only change this if your administrator tells you to. **Save and test** checks the connection. |
| **Open help** | Opens the built-in help. |

### Changing your password

Settings > **Change password**. Enter your current password, then a new one (at least 8 characters with an uppercase letter,
a lowercase letter and a number) twice. Tap **Change password**.

### Signing out

Settings > **Sign out** > confirm. You are also signed out automatically if your session expires (after 8 hours).

## 5. Help inside the app

Tap **Help** on the sign-in screen, or Settings > **Open help**. Tap any question to open its answer.

## 6. Keeping your information safe

- Sign out on shared phones.
- Never share your password. Administrators will never ask for it.
- The app stores your sign-in in encrypted storage and removes it when you sign out.

## 7. Messages you might see

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| "Your session has expired. Please sign in again." | You were signed out for safety. Sign in again. |
| "You don't have permission to do that." | You tried to open something that belongs to another teacher, school or family. |
| "This app is for teachers and parents..." | Administrators and coordinators use the web portal, not this app. |
| "Attendance cannot be recorded for a session that has not happened yet." | Wait until the day of the session. |
| "The teacher already has a session that overlaps this time." | You already have a session at that time. Pick another time. |
| "An account with this email address already exists..." | Sign in instead of creating a new account. |
| "The server took too long to respond..." | The server may be waking up. Wait a moment and try again. |

## 8. Troubleshooting: "Can't reach the server"

1. Check your phone has mobile data or Wi-Fi (try opening a website).
2. On the sign-in screen tap **Server settings** and check the address with your administrator.
3. Tap **Save and test**. A green message with "Connected" means it works.
4. The server can take up to a minute to wake up if nobody used it for a while - wait and try again.

## 9. Installing the app (APK)

1. Download `app-debug.apk` (from the GitHub **Actions > latest run > Artifacts > lpms-teacher-debug-apk**, or from your coordinator).
2. Open the file on the phone. If Android asks, allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser or Files app.
3. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.
